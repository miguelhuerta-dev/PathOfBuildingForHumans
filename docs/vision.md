# Vision

**Same builds. A deeper understanding.**

Path of Building tells you what the numbers are. Path of Building for Humans
tells you what they mean and what to do next, in plain words, while you play.

## Who it is for

- New and returning players who follow a guide and want to know whether their
  character is on track.
- Players who can read Path of Building but want the answer, not the
  spreadsheet: what is wrong, what fixes it, what to buy or take next.
- Spanish speakers, who get the whole app in Spanish (game names stay as the
  game writes them).

## What it promises

1. **A verdict, not a sheet.** One sentence on where the build stands, the
   problems that matter worst first, and the next step worth taking.
2. **The real numbers.** Every figure comes from Path of Building Community's
   own engine, run on the player's real character. Nothing is estimated by
   hand.
3. **The guide, stage by stage.** Gear, gem links and where each gem comes
   from, ascendancy order, bandits, pantheon, passive tree and Atlas, for the
   stage the player is at.
4. **Live while playing.** The character refreshes when the player changes
   zone; the guide follows them.

## Principles

- **Close the loop in the game.** A feature earns its place when it changes
  what the player does next in the game.
- **Within GGG's rules, always.** Only public profiles and official exports. No
  reading the game's memory or network traffic, nothing that could get an
  account banned.
- **GGG's art stays GGG's.** Icons and item art are read from the player's own
  installation, never shipped or downloaded.
- **Honest about limits.** When something cannot be done (the Atlas is not in
  public profiles), the app says why instead of guessing.
- **One file, no setup.** A single portable Windows app.
- **Its own look.** The Atlas Archivist style (iron, blood and gold) is
  described in [`visual_language.md`](visual_language.md).

## Not the goal

- Replacing Path of Building for theorycrafting. Builds are still made there.
- Automation of any kind inside the game.
- Selling anything. The app is free and MIT licensed.
