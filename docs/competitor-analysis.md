# Competitor analysis

The tools a Path of Exile player already uses, what each does best, and where
Path of Building for Humans fits. Most are complements, not rivals: the app
builds on several of them.

| Tool | What it does best | What it leaves to the player | How we relate |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Path of Building Community](https://pathofbuilding.community/) | Exact calculation of every damage and defence interaction; the standard for making builds | Reading hundreds of numbers and deciding what they mean and what to do next | **Built on it.** Its engine runs headless, unmodified, on the player's real character |
| Build sharing sites ([pobb.in](https://pobb.in/), pastebin, pob.codes, rentry, poedb) | Sharing a build as a link | Comparing the shared build with your own character | **Guide source.** A shared link opens as a guide, compared stage by stage |
| Guide sites ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe) and others) | Written guides with leveling stages and Path of Building codes | Checking your character against the guide while you play | **Guide source.** A Maxroll link loads directly; the app follows the guide's stages and points out what is missing |
| [exile-leveling](https://github.com/HeartofPhos/exile-leveling) | The campaign route and where each gem is handed out | Linking that to the build you follow | **Data source** (MIT). Gem quest and vendor sources come from its export |
| [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/) | Economy prices and popular builds per league | Knowing which of those changes would help your character | **Price and guide source:** unique prices in the Forge, and its build links open as guides |
| GGG's passive and Atlas tree viewers | The official trees and their share links | Seeing progress against a guide | **Official exports.** Trees are drawn from GGG's own data; Atlas links are read in GGG's format |

## What only Path of Building for Humans does

- **Answers instead of numbers:** a verdict, the Threat Register (worst problem
  first, with its cost and fix) and the next upgrade, measured by Path of
  Building.
- **The guide, live:** the character refreshes when the player changes zone,
  and the guide's gear, gems, tree and Atlas follow the stage they are at.
- **Within the rules:** public profiles and official exports only; the game's
  art read from the player's own installation.
- **In Spanish too,** with a tour for new players.

## Where others are ahead

- **Theorycrafting:** Path of Building stays the tool to make a build from
  scratch.
- **Economy:** poe.ninja covers far more of the market.
- **Platforms:** the app is Windows only for now.
